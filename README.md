# NW-Clothing-Images
I have taken pictures of everything visible on a ped in FiveM, including clothing, tattoos, hair, etc. The pictures are not perfect, and around 2–0.5% of them may contain small errors or imperfections.

Also credits to https://github.com/Bentix-cs/fivem-greenscreener, without him. This wouldn't be possible. I used the MLO/Greenscreen, and parts of their code, i just modified it and made it work faster 10x. All images took about 15/30 min.
